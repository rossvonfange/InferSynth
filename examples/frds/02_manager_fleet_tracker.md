Team,

Per the leadership offsite, we are greenlit for the trailer-tracking pilot.
Requirements below. Legal has reviewed nothing yet; assume they will.

1. BUSINESS NEED. We lose ~$40k/yr to misplaced trailers across 3 depots.
   The device must let dispatch see trailer location "within a parking lot."
2. The unit must report position at least hourly, more often when moving.
3. Battery life is the #1 complaint from the vendor units we trialed. Target
   is a full season (call it 6 months) without a charge. Non-negotiable.
4. It must survive being outside. Trailers get pressure-washed.
5. Unit cost must land under $35 at 500 units or the ROI case collapses.
6. It should not require our drivers to do anything. Ever. If a driver has
   to press a button, the project fails.
7. Timeline: pilot hardware in hand by end of Q3.

I do not care how it works. I care that Maria in dispatch can find trailer
#4471 on a Tuesday. Please advise on feasibility and what I forgot to ask for.

— J.M.
